Privacy Policy

Kalyvor Invoice · Effective date: November 10, 2026

Kalyvor Invoice ("we", "our", or "the app") is a business invoicing application developed and published under the Kalyvor brand.

This Privacy Policy explains how Kalyvor Invoice handles information when you use our Android application.

1. Information You Enter

Kalyvor Invoice allows you to store business and customer information, including:

  • Business names and contact details.
  • Customer names, email addresses, phone numbers and addresses.
  • Invoice, estimate and receipt details.
  • Products, services, prices, taxes and discounts.
  • Payment records, balances and transaction references.
  • Business logos, notes and payment instructions.

This information is stored locally on your device to provide the application's features. We do not operate an account-based cloud service for storing your invoice records.

2. Local Storage and Backups

Your business records are stored on your Android device. Depending on your Android device and backup settings, Android Auto Backup may include eligible application data in your Google account backup.

Backup handling is subject to your device settings and Google's services. We recommend keeping your device secure and maintaining your own copies of important business documents.

3. Advertising

Kalyvor Invoice may display advertisements through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and device identifiers.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • App interactions and advertising interactions.
  • Diagnostics and performance information.

Advertising data handling is subject to Google's privacy policies and applicable consent requirements. You can manage available advertising privacy choices through the app's privacy options, where provided.

4. Subscription and Payments

Kalyvor Invoice offers optional Pro subscriptions through Google Play Billing.

Subscription purchases, payment processing, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled by Google Play under its applicable terms and privacy policy. We do not collect or store your full payment card details.

5. Sharing Documents

You can choose to share invoices, estimates and receipts using other applications, including messaging and email apps.

When you choose to share a document, the receiving application and its provider handle the information according to their own privacy policies. Kalyvor Invoice does not control how third-party applications process shared documents.

6. Permissions

The app requests access only where needed for its features. For example, you may select a business logo using Android's photo picker. The app may also use system document-sharing functionality to export PDF files.

Advertising and billing SDKs may add permissions necessary for their operation.

7. Data Security

We use Android platform security features and secure network connections for supported online services.

Your locally stored business records are protected by your device's security mechanisms. They are not independently encrypted by an application-level database encryption system.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

8. Children's Privacy

Kalyvor Invoice is designed for business users and is not intended for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Business records remain on your device until you delete them or remove the application and its associated data.

Eligible Android backups may remain subject to your Google account backup settings and Google's retention policies. Information processed by Google AdMob and Google Play is subject to Google's applicable data retention and deletion policies.

10. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP)
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Terms of Service
  • Google AdMob Help

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's functionality, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
